⚠️ CRITICAL DIC, FFP, CRYOPRECIPITATE, TRANEXAMIC ACID (TXA) & JUGULAR VENIPUNCTURE GUARD: (1) Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP 10–20 mL/kg IV over 3–4h) replenishes labile & stable clotting factors (II, V, VII, VIII, IX, X) and Antithrombin III (< 65% indicates severe consumption); (2) Cryoprecipitate (1 unit/10 kg IV) is indicated for severe hypofibrinogenemia (Fibrinogen < 100 mg/dL) or von Willebrand Factor (vWF) / Factor VIII deficiency; (3) Tranexamic Acid (TXA 10 mg/kg IV slow over 15–20 min q8h) or Epsilon-Aminocaproic Acid (EACA 50–100 mg/kg IV/PO q8h) inhibits plasminogen activation in Greyhound/Sighthound Delayed Post-Operative Bleeding & Trauma Hyperfibrinolysis (TEG LY30 > 3%); (4) STRICT CONTRAINDICATION — NEVER perform Jugular Venipuncture, non-compressible central neck lines, cystocentesis, or IM injections in coagulopathic patients (risk of fatal compressive cervical airway hematoma; use peripheral saphenous/cephalic veins with pressure wraps only).
